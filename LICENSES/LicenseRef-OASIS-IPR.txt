tests/data/sarif-schema-2.1.0.json is the JSON schema of the Static Analysis Results
Interchange Format (SARIF) Version 2.1.0, Errata 01, an OASIS Standard, copied
unmodified from https://github.com/oasis-tcs/sarif-spec (sarif-2.1/schema/) to
validate the SARIF report in tests.
