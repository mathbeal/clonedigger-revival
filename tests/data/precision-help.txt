usage: precision [-h] [--sample SAMPLE] [--labels LABELS]

Measure precision on the labelled sample, with its Wilson interval.

options:
  -h, --help       show this help message and exit
  --sample SAMPLE  the pairs drawn, as a report of pairs (default benchmarks/sample.json)
  --labels LABELS  the verdicts given on them, as JSON Lines (default benchmarks/labels.jsonl)
